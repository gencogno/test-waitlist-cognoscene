the problem · how it begins

unexpected impulses — mostly from social feeds. influencers selling you gadgets you don't need, still making you feel like you're missing out. you end up buying not just the gadget — but the case, the bundle, the accessories. shopping platforms are built to keep you spending.

“literally wasted nearly s$230 on clothes because of fomo.”— adam, founder of cognoscene

and you'll do it again. despite controlling yourself, the dopamine from social media and shopping makes checkout feel irresistible.

there's nothing to interrupt the urges when it hits you, leaving you prone to financially vulnerabilities & unnecessary purchase guilt.

hence, we built an extension to prevent you from shopping destructively using friction.
